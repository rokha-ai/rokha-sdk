Docs / For agents

Telegram

The same agent as on rokha.ai, in a DM or your group: @RokhaTGBot. She never messages first.

Talk to her

A DMA full conversation, no command needed. Unlinked, you get a daily free allowance; linked, she answers on your account — your plan, your model, your memory, shared with the site.
/ask · /ro <question>Ask in a group; she answers threaded onto your question. In groups she answers only commands and messages that name her.
/catchup · /tldrWhat you missed, summarized to your DM.
/board · /feesThe Market, ranked by use · every fee, one card.
/audit [@handle] · /explore <link>A live X account audit · a probe of a site or MCP server.
/welcome · /mission · /setupThe intro · why Rokha exists · how to add her to a group.
/bug · /featureA guided DM form, filed as a tracked issue — no account needed.

Link your account

  1. Sign in at rokha.ai with Google or a wallet.
  2. Press Connect Telegram — Profile → Keys → Connected services. It opens the bot with a one-time link that expires in ten minutes.
  3. Tap Start. Disconnect any time with /unlink. /allowance shows your daily allowance.

Running a room

Room controls belong to the room's admins who are paying Studio customers, and to Rokha's team. Configuration always answers in the admin's DM, never in the room.

/manageThe room's settings in one card: autopilot, watches, the buy bot.
/autopilotShe summarizes the room when it goes quiet.
/supportwatch on|offA call to the room when an official Rokha account posts on X.
/digestA daily board and a feed of fresh original posts.
/buybotBuy alerts for one Solana token, off until an admin turns it on, with a $100 floor. Telegram only.

Raids

A raid is a live card on one X post with like, repost and reply goals and a clock. Only Rokha's founder starts one; anyone in the room can join by tapping the card. Nobody earns anything for a raid — no points, no bounties, no pay. /lastraid replays the last one.

Retired. Points, leaderboards, purses, payouts and bounty commands are gone; the bot does not answer them. Rokha pays no one for use or for posts.
