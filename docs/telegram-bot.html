Rokha pays for use, never for posts. Three ways to earn: be one of the week's top 10 Members on the board (USDC every Friday 22:00 UTC — 1st $30 · 2nd $20 · 3rd $16 · 4th–10th $12), sell what you build (80% yours), or the affiliate program (25% of a referred buyer's first Studio month). Payouts are USDC, on-chain, receipted.
