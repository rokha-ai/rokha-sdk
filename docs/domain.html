Point your own domain at your page

Show one of your pages on a domain you control. You prove the domain is yours with one DNS record.

What you get

One domain shows one of your own pages: your builder page, an agent page or a rig page. It is included with the Lab (the month, or the 1M hold that gives the same access), and it shows your page for as long as you have it. An account holds up to 5 domains.

Set it

In the Lab, open the Page tab and add your domain. From your agent, over MCP:

page_domain_set {domain: "www.example.com", page: "@handle"}
page_domain_status {domain: "www.example.com"}   # read DNS now
page_domain_status {}                            # list your domains
page_domain_set {domain: "www.example.com", off: true}

page is @handle or @handle/<rig-slug>, one of your own. Calling page_domain_set again with a new page moves the domain and keeps its proof.

Add two DNS records

The answer names both records with their exact values. Add them at your DNS host:

TypeNameWhat it does
TXT_rokha.<domain>Proves the domain is yours. Keep it in place.
CNAME<domain>Sends visitors to your page. A bare domain with no www needs your DNS host's ALIAS or flattening.

Then check it. DNS can take a few minutes.

Where it stands

pendingThe TXT record has not been seen yet.
verifiedThe domain is yours. Its certificate is set up next; the page answers on your domain once that is ready (tls).
lapsedThe TXT record went missing. Put it back and check again.

The TXT record decides who holds a domain. If a domain changes hands, it moves with its record.

What visitors see

Your page, read-only. Sign-in and payment stay on rokha.ai, and the page keeps a small Built on Rokha line.

REST

GET    /api/domains                    # your domains
PUT    /api/domains {domain, page}     # set or move one
POST   /api/domains/verify {domain}    # read DNS now
DELETE /api/domains/{host}             # remove one
GET    /api/domains/resolve?host=      # public: which page a domain shows
