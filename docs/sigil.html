Sigil

Rokha's blockchain tooling for agents. Read any token, its holders and launch, what an exit really returns, and any wallet's holdings, trades and PnL.

What it is

Sigil is a set of read tools Rokha runs itself. Each one is an MCP tool and a REST door with the same name. Nothing is signed and nothing moves. To trade, use Signet: it swaps and places limit orders on your own wallet, only under a mandate you granted.

MCP:   https://rokha.ai/mcp/jsonrpc?toolset=sigil
REST:  POST https://rokha.ai/api/sigil/<tool>   {arguments}

The tool name works with or without the sigil_ prefix on REST: /api/sigil/token and /api/sigil/sigil_token are the same door.

The tools

ToolArgumentsWhat it answers
sigil_tokentoken, chain?On Solana: mint and freeze authority, the top holder's and top ten's share, and the market (price, liquidity, FDV, volume, pair age). On Base, Ethereum, BNB Chain or Robinhood Chain (chain base, ethereum, bsc, robinhood): the owner and whether it renounced, whether it is an upgradeable proxy, owner powers found in the bytecode (mint, pause, blacklist, fee and trading switches), and the market.
sigil_token_holderstoken, limit?A Solana token's largest holders (up to 20), each one's share of supply, and a name for wallets Rokha tracks.
sigil_token_launchtokenWho launched a Solana token: the deployer wallet, its age and earlier launches, snipers, same-slot buyers, the dev's share, and whether the snipers still hold.
sigil_exit_quotetoken, usd?What selling usd worth (default 1,000) of a Solana token returns now, from a live Jupiter quote into USDC: what you get, the loss against the market price, and the price impact.
sigil_walletwalletA Solana wallet's holdings and their value, how old it is, and which wallets funded it.
sigil_wallet_tradeswallet, limit?Its recent swaps (up to 50), newest first: what it gave, what it got, when and where.
sigil_wallet_pnlwallet, pages?Realised PnL, win rate and median hold time, computed by Rokha from up to 200 recent swaps, per token too.

An example

curl -s -X POST https://rokha.ai/api/sigil/exit_quote \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $ROKHA_TOKEN" -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"token":"DezXAZ8z7PnrnRJjz3wXBoRgixCa6xjnB7YaB1pPB263","usd":5000}'

Every answer carries tool, as_of and by. A read of public data, never advice.

How the numbers are made

  • PnL is average cost per token, in SOL. Swaps paid in USDC or USDT are converted at today's SOL price, and the answer says so. If a wallet sells tokens it bought before the window Rokha read, that sale never counts as profit (sold_from_before_window). Token-to-token swaps carry no price and are counted as swaps_unpriced.
  • Owner powers on an EVM token are functions found in its bytecode. Finding one is a sign, not proof: the function may be guarded or never used. No sell is simulated, so a honeypot is never ruled out (not_checked).
  • Holders are the largest token accounts. A pool or an exchange can sit among them.

Price

The Lab includes 300 Sigil reads a day. A signed-in account gets one free read a day, then $0.02 a read, sold as a pack of 50 for $1. Buy the pack with product_checkout; its product id is GET /api/products?kind=runs&ref=rokha-sigil. A read that fails hands its run back. All payments are final.

Refusals

400bad_address, bad_chain, bad_usd, solana_only: refused before anything is spent.
402purchase_required: the price, the free read and how to buy the pack.
429sigil_cap: today's reads are spent. It names the way up.
502not_a_mint, not_a_contract, no_price, node_error: the read failed and its run was handed back.
