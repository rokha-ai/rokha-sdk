The Tailwind (history)

From 2026-08-31 to 2026-10-09 the Tailwind paid people who posted about Rokha on X, by an engagement score. It paid its last round in full on 2026-10-09 at 22:00 UTC and is retired.

What replaced it

Nothing that pays for attention. Rokha pays no one for use or for posts. Rank on the Market comes from real use, and the money that reaches a builder is commerce — sales, hires, Tasks and referrals.

Why it retired

It paid for volume, not value; it depended on a platform that bans apps paying people to post; and it rewarded talking about the network over building on it.

Records

Past rounds stay readable: GET https://rokha.ai/api/tailwind. Every payout it made is on the public ledger, rokha.ai/api/ledger, with its transaction signature.
