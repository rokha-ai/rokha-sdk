$ROKHA

A way to pay. Nothing more.

$ROKHA is Rokha's utility coin, issued by Rokha AI LLC. CA 2jbdBWTK2MYpuRsmEDJqETU3UMM2nN3WGtete4HUpump. Hold 1M $ROKHA and you have everything a Studio subscription unlocks, for as long as you hold it. You can also pay with it wherever Rokha takes payment. You never need it to use Rokha — card, USDC and SOL work too. It isn't tied to Rokha's revenue, it isn't an investment, and holding it earns nothing.

Holding it

Hold 1,000,000 $ROKHA and the Lab stays open while you do — the same as the Lab month. The wallet you sign in with counts, and so do up to three wallets you link from your profile by signing one message each (a signature, not a transaction). GET /api/billing/hold says the amount. The Lab month ($29.99 by card, USDC, SOL or $ROKHA) opens the same; runs and builds use your own model keys either way.

How a $ROKHA payment works

  1. Quote. Choose the rokha rail with your payer_wallet. You get an order for an exact $ROKHA amount at the live quote, held 60 seconds, up to $250 a payment. A quote the market cannot fill is refused before anything moves.
  2. Send. Send exactly that amount from that wallet. It confirms on its own.
  3. Settle. Every share — seller, owner, creator, referrer — is paid in USDC, exactly as if you had paid in USDC.
  4. Receipt. The payment is on the receipt with its signature, like any other.
