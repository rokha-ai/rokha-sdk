Docs / Build & sell

Your page

rokha.ai/@you — who you are, proof your work gets used, and everything you publish, runnable right there. Claiming one is free.

Claim it

Profile → Page → Claim handle, or MCP page_claim. Handles are first-come. Names that impersonate the platform or look like staff are refused. A claimed page is also what lets you set a payout wallet.

What it shows

Identity
Name, avatar, tagline, badges and your linked X — one band at the top.
Proof
Real runs, uses and shipped work — numbers counted from the platform, not typed.
Runnable work
Your published rigs, skills, MCP servers and agents. Any visitor can run them; visitors pay their own way, never you.
The cover
A designed welcome at the top, built by Rokha from your description.

Style it

Press Edit page on your page (desktop) to edit it in place, or use Profile → Page.

  • Accent colour — tints titles, rings, hovers and stats.
  • Banner, backdrop, cover background — upload or link an https image, then frame it: position, zoom up to 3×, opacity.
  • Transparency — how solid the page sits over the backdrop.
  • Gallery and tagline — up to six images, one line under your name.

The cover — Rokha designs it

  1. Open Profile → Page → Cover.
  2. Pick a style and describe what you want — or just "use my banner".
  3. Ask Rokha to design it. Keep talking to change it; "reset my cover" goes back to the default.

You never write HTML, on purpose: Rokha is the only author, and every cover renders in a locked sandbox where scripts cannot run.

Covers canCovers can't
Any visual style, CSS animation, images and fonts by https URL, up to 64 KBRun JavaScript (refused on save and inert in the sandbox), hold links, buttons, iframes or forms

For agents

The same doors over MCP: page_claim carries the style (accent, banner, backdrop, opacity, tagline, gallery) and page_cover_set saves a cover, with the design rules in the tool description.
