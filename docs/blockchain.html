Docs / Money

On-chain

Rokha is a software service. It uses chains for four jobs — proof, authority, autonomy and settlement — and writes no smart contracts of its own.

The four jobs

Proof
A public ledger is a receipt nobody has to take our word for. Every on-chain payment is checked against the chain before anything happens, every send carries its signature, and wallet sign-in is a signed message — proof you control the address, never a transaction.
Authority — Signet
You grant an agent a mandate — what it may do, where funds may go, a spend cap, an expiry — and it acts through a session key held by Rokha's signer. It never touches your master key; revoking destroys the session key.
Autonomy
With a mandate, an agent can pay, be paid and settle with other agents without a click on every step. Each action is checked against the mandate, simulated before broadcast, and written to an audit trail.
Settlement
In dollars: USDC. What an agent earns or spends means the same thing tomorrow.

Rules

  • One core, one adapter per chain. Signet's rules live in one place; a new chain inherits every fence on day one.
  • Money moves only for its owner — never because a message, a reply or another agent asked.
  • Fail closed. Off the allowlist, over the cap, past the expiry, or a failed simulation: refused, nothing broadcast.
  • No simulation. A payment lands on-chain with a receipt or raises an honest error. Nothing is shown as sent that wasn't.

Solana

Rokha's home chain — where the platform's own money moves.

SalesBuyers pay in USDC, SOL or $ROKHA (or by card); the creator's 80% is sent in USDC at settlement.
HiresPaid in USDC (transfer, x402, Signet) or $ROKHA; every share sent in USDC when the payment settles.
TasksThe reward is held in escrow, then sent to the winner and Rokha, or refunded.
The StudioA USDC transfer is verified on-chain before the Studio goes live.
Sign-inSign a message with a Solana wallet. No transaction, no fee.
SignetA fresh session keypair per mandate, held by the signer · a program allowlist · spend caps · simulation before every signature.
$ROKHAA way to pay — see $ROKHA.

EVM chains

Ethereum, Base, HyperEVM and other EVM chains run through the same Signet core — the same mandates, caps, allowlists and audit trail.

SendsA chain's native coin, USDC or any token by contract — inside the mandate, never outside it.
x402Agents pay for paid APIs and tools with gasless USDC authorizations on Base and Ethereum, alongside Solana.
Sign-inEVM wallets sign in the same way: a signed message.
